LINEWORK.
This is a line-driven style: the outlines are the subject. Draw long, deliberate, slightly
tapered ink lines in a deep cool near-black (#1C2224), with confident sweeping curves and sharp
angular corners; let a few lines break and re-enter for rhythm. Line colour shifts subtly along
its length -- warmer in the lit arc, cooler in the shadow arc -- instead of one flat black.
Line weight varies boldly: heavy on the outer silhouette and the mint accent band, hairline on
the inner rules.

STYLE.
Flat, poster-like Japanese illustration with a graphic-design sensibility: large bold colour
blocks, a deliberately limited palette, high immediate impact, no blending and no soft gradients.
Shapes are clean and a little exaggerated. Minimal shading -- at most one blocked-in shadow tone
per surface. The result should read like a striking screen-printed poster of a card table:
cool, sharp and stylish. Illustrated, not photorealistic, not 3D, not vector-smooth corporate.

KEY.
Everything outside the complete tabletop silhouette is one perfectly flat solid pure white
(#FFFFFF) background: uniform, with no shadow, gradient, texture, glow, floor or scene. The
table's outline must be closed and cleanly drawn so the white can be separated from it.
Leave a small even white margin; do not crop the oval.

AVOID.
No words, letters, digits, Chinese characters, runes, labels, logos, signatures or watermarks.
No extra objects: no cards, chips, coins, dice, hands, people, bottles or chairs. No scene or
background except flat magenta; no reflections or cast shadows outside the tabletop. No 3D,

corporate design, not sci-fi UI.
